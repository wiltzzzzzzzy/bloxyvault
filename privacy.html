1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information BloxyVault ("BloxyVault," "we," "us," or "our") collects when you use bloxyvaults.com (the "Site"), how that information is used, and what rights you may have over it.

BloxyVault is a Roblox-pet-themed gaming site, based in Luxembourg and available to users worldwide. Users verify ownership of a Roblox account to log in, then play games, trade, and interact with other users using an in-site virtual currency ("CF Coins") and virtual items.

BloxyVault is operated by an individual, not by a registered company or separate legal entity. "BloxyVault" is the name under which the Site is operated and the name to use when contacting us.

Because we are based in Luxembourg, an EU member state, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies to how we process your personal data, regardless of where you're located. If you're outside the EU, you may also have rights under your own local law, in addition to what's described here.

2. Age Requirement

BloxyVault is intended only for users aged 18 or older. By using the Site, you confirm that you are at least 18 years old. BloxyVault does not knowingly permit use by anyone under 18.

Our login process verifies that you own the Roblox account you're signing in with — it does not independently verify your age. If you believe someone under 18 is using BloxyVault, please contact us on Discord (§13) so we can look into it.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Roblox Account Information

When you verify your account with BloxyVault, we process:

  • Your Roblox username
  • Your Roblox user ID

To confirm you own the Roblox account you're claiming, we may temporarily check the text of your public Roblox profile bio for a one-time verification code you're asked to place there. This bio text is read at the moment of verification only — it is not stored afterward; only the pass/fail result matters going forward.

We do not request, receive, or store your Roblox password. Verification works either through the bio-code method above, or through Roblox's own official "Sign in with Roblox" (OAuth) login, where Roblox itself handles your credentials and only shares your username with us.

3.2 BloxyVault Account & Game Data

Once you're logged in, we store the following, tied to your username:

  • Username
  • Roblox user ID (associated at verification)
  • CF Coin balance
  • Virtual item inventory
  • Game statistics (amounts wagered, won, and lost)
  • Game history (e.g. Coinflip history, including opponents, items, and outcomes)
  • Withdrawal requests (requests to convert in-site items back to real Roblox items)
  • Transaction records (an internal log of balance/inventory changes)
  • Notifications (e.g. "your withdrawal was fulfilled," "you won a Case Battle")

3.3 Information We Do Not Collect

BloxyVault does not collect or process:

  • Credit or debit card information
  • Real-money payment information of any kind
  • Robux or other Roblox payment information
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • A BloxyVault account password (there isn't one — login works entirely through Roblox verification)
  • Your Roblox password

BloxyVault does not have any real-money purchase or payment functionality. Any exchange of in-site items for real Roblox items is handled manually by an administrator, outside of any automated payment system.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Where the GDPR applies, here is why we process your data:

  • Roblox verification, account data, balance, inventory, game history, transaction records, withdrawal requests, and notifications are processed because they're necessary to provide the service you've signed up to use.
  • Session tokens and localStorage login persistence are processed because they're necessary to keep you logged in, which is functionality you're actively using.
  • Duplicate-account detection, an internal process that consolidates accounts differing only by capitalization, is processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping account records accurate and preventing abuse.

We do not use your information for marketing, analytics, or advertising, and none of your data is processed on that basis.

5. Browser Storage

BloxyVault does not use cookies, sessionStorage, or IndexedDB.

We use your browser's localStorage to store two small pieces of information on your own device:

  • bloxyvault_session — contains your username, a session token, and a timestamp. This keeps you logged in across visits without needing to re-verify every time. It's designed to expire automatically after 7 days, at which point you'll be asked to verify again.
  • bv_sidenav_collapsed — a simple interface preference (whether your sidebar is collapsed), containing no personal information.

This data stays on your own device. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for bloxyvaults.com.

6. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Verify that you own the Roblox account you're logging in with
  • Maintain your BloxyVault balance, inventory, and game statistics
  • Keep you signed in between visits
  • Process withdrawal requests (converting in-site items back to real Roblox items)
  • Show you your own transaction and notification history
  • Operate core game features (Coinflip, Case Battles, Mines, Sink, Jackpot, Exchange, etc.)
  • Maintain an internal record of account activity for fraud prevention, dispute resolution, and account integrity — for example, detecting duplicate accounts
  • Display public information such as leaderboards and chat, as described in §7

7. Chat and Public Information

7.1 Chat

BloxyVault has a live chat feature. Chat messages are:

  • Broadcast to other users currently on the Site
  • Kept in a rolling history of the most recent 100 messages on our server — older messages are automatically dropped as new ones arrive
  • Not stored in our database. Chat history exists only in server memory and is lost whenever the server restarts.

Because chat is visible to other users by design, please don't share personal information in chat messages.

7.2 What Other Users Can See

Depending on the feature you're using, other users on BloxyVault may see:

  • Your username
  • Your public game statistics (amounts wagered/won/lost) and leaderboard position
  • Certain live game information (e.g. your side and items in an active Coinflip, live Case Battle activity, a live feed of recent wins)
  • Your chat messages, while chat history is available

Other users are not shown, through the Site's normal features:

  • Your CF Coin balance
  • Your full inventory
  • Your withdrawal requests
  • Your transaction history
  • Your notifications

8. Service Providers / Third Parties

BloxyVault communicates with the following external services:

Roblox (users.roblox.com, thumbnails.roblox.com, apis.roblox.com) — used to verify your Roblox account, look up your avatar image, and (if you use "Sign in with Roblox") complete OAuth login. We send your Roblox username and/or user ID to these services as part of these normal operations.

Railway — our hosting provider, which runs our application server and PostgreSQL database. Our own application code does not read, log, or store visitors' IP addresses — but as our infrastructure provider, Railway may independently process connection-level information (such as IP addresses) as a normal part of hosting the Site.

Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) — we load web fonts from Google's servers. When your browser does this, it communicates directly with Google's infrastructure, which may expose ordinary connection information (such as your IP address) to Google, subject to Google's own privacy policy.

We do not use analytics platforms, tracking pixels, advertising SDKs, error-tracking services, or social media tracking pixels.

9. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your information. We do not share your account data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Information may be shared:

  • With Roblox, as part of the verification/login process described in §3.1 and §8
  • With Railway, as our hosting/database provider, as part of normal Site operation
  • Publicly with other users, to the extent described in §7.2
  • If required to comply with a legal obligation, or to protect the security or integrity of the Site

10. Data Retention

We do not have a defined retention schedule for account data. Balance, inventory, statistics, game history, transaction records, withdrawal requests, and notifications persist in our database for as long as your account exists.

Some data clears automatically regardless:

  • Chat messages (rolling 100-message limit, cleared entirely on server restart — §7.1)
  • Your login session (expires after 7 days, both on our server and in your browser's localStorage)
  • Roblox bio verification codes (checked once, never stored — §3.1)

BloxyVault does not offer, and does not intend to offer, a self-service "Delete Account" feature.

About the "Reset Account" feature: BloxyVault's system includes a limited, developer-facing feature that — where enabled — lets a logged-in user reset their own balance to the starting amount and empty their current inventory. This is not account deletion and does not remove your historical data. When used:

  • Balance resets to the default starting amount and inventory is emptied
  • Underlying inventory records are set to zero rather than removed
  • The account itself, statistics, transaction history, coinflip history, withdrawal history, and notifications all remain
  • A record of the reset is added to the transaction history

This feature is not intended for general public use and is not a substitute for exercising any data rights you may have (see §11).

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights that can include: access to your data, correction of inaccurate data, restriction of processing, objection to processing, data portability, and erasure ("the right to be forgotten"). Where the GDPR applies to you, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Luxembourg, that's the Commission Nationale pour la Protection des Données (CNPD) — regardless of how we respond to a request you make to us directly.

BloxyVault does not currently have a general technical process — automated or manual — for deleting or anonymizing all of a user's stored data on request. This means that if you ask us to delete or otherwise act on your data, we may not currently be able to fully carry that out for every record, even where you have a legal right to ask. That limitation doesn't take away any right you may have under applicable law, and erasure itself isn't an unconditional right in the first place — whether, and how, a particular request can be granted depends on the circumstances.

If you'd like to make a request regarding your data, or have questions about how BloxyVault handles it, contact us on Discord (§13). We'll respond directly and explain what we're able to do.

12. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your account:

  • Access to your account requires a valid session token issued only after successful Roblox verification
  • Administrative actions (balance adjustments, withdrawal processing, account lookups) are restricted to the designated administrator account, enforced on our server rather than only hidden in the interface
  • Notification and account data are scoped to the logged-in user making the request, so that normal use of the Site can't read or modify another user's private account data
  • We do not store Roblox or BloxyVault passwords, because BloxyVault does not use passwords
  • Server credentials (such as our database connection and OAuth secrets) are kept server-side and are never exposed to your browser

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee that unauthorized access will never occur.
